Simple Sandglass

A classic wooden hourglass for iPhone. Set the time, flip the phone, watch the sand fall.

How to use it

  1. Drag the wooden slider to set the time, from 10 seconds to 30 minutes. The default is 3 minutes.
  2. Turn your iPhone upside down. The sand starts to flow and the slider label shows the time left.
  3. When the sand runs out, a chime rings with vibration. Tap anywhere to stop it.
  4. Turn the phone back while the sand is falling and it flows the other way, like a real hourglass.

Questions

The sand doesn’t start when I flip the phone.

Hold the phone upright, then turn it fully upside down so the top edge points to the floor. Lying flat on a table keeps the current state. Portrait Orientation Lock does not matter.

I can’t move the slider.

The time can be changed only while the sand is not falling. Wait for the end or turn the phone back until the top bulb is empty.

I didn’t hear the chime.

The chime plays even in silent mode, but it follows the media volume. Turn the volume up with the side buttons while the app is open.

No alert came while I used another app.

Allow notifications for the app in Settings → Notifications. In the background the app sends a notification instead of the chime.

Does the screen stay on?

Yes, while the sand is falling and while the chime rings. Otherwise the screen locks as usual.

Privacy Policy

Simple Sandglass does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

  • The selected time is saved only on your device.
  • Motion sensor data is used only to detect when the phone is turned over and never leaves the device.
  • Notifications are local and scheduled by your device.
  • The app contains no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.
